Ward meeting Grassroots

Meeting with Aye Ward I

Attendees: Dr. Emmanuel Feyisayo, Okitipupa Youth Leader, and members of the Fadope family.

The meeting was held under the leadership and coordination of the Ward Leader. Dr. Emmanuel Feyisayo was formally introduced to the gathering, after which he addressed the attendees and communicated his political ambition to represent Okitipupa Constituency II at the Ondo State House of Assembly.

Following his address, the women of the ward gathered around him in a show of solidarity. They sang songs of encouragement and offered prayers for him, his aspirations, and the success of his political journey.

Outcome

The meeting provided an opportunity to engage with members of the ward, strengthen grassroots relationships, and foster goodwill and support for his aspiration.

Endorsement Courtesy visit

Courtesy visit to the Halubi Family

Purpose: Engagement with the Halubi Family regarding the interest of Hon. Emmanuel Feyisayo Fadope to contest for the Ondo State House of Assembly, Okitipupa Constituency II, under the ADC.

The delegation paid a courtesy visit to members of the Halubi Family to formally communicate his interest in contesting. Hon. Emmanuel Fadope was given the opportunity to address the family, where he shared his intentions, outlined his commitment to the constituency, and assured them that he would remain accessible and committed to them beyond the election.

Outcome

Following the engagement and discussions, the Halubi Family endorsed Hon. Emmanuel Feyisayo Fadope’s candidacy. The family also encouraged its members to share the aspiration with their spouses, children and other relatives, and to support the ADC at the polls.

Endorsement Courtesy visit

Visit to the Ajaka of Idepe Family

Purpose: A delegation led by Elder A.O. Fadope paid a courtesy visit to the Ajaka of Idepe Family to formally communicate Hon. Emmanuel Fadope’s interest in contesting for the Ondo State House of Assembly, Okitipupa Constituency II, under the platform of the African Democratic Congress (ADC). The Petu of Idepe, alongside other notable dignitaries and members of the family, was present.

The meeting gave the delegation the opportunity to formally present the aspiration and seek the family’s support and endorsement. Elder Fadope Amos addressed the gathering and passionately appealed for support for his son, Hon. Emmanuel Feyisayo Fadope. Hon. Emmanuel Fadope then spoke on his aspiration, his commitment to the constituency, and his determination to serve the people if elected.

At the close of the meeting he received the full endorsement of the family, and was prayed for with prayers for wisdom, favour, success and a successful political journey. The Petu of Idepe further encouraged members of the family to share the aspiration with their spouses, children and other relatives, and to support Hon. Emmanuel Fadope and the ADC at the polls.

Outcome

The visit was successful. The Ajaka of Idepe Family formally expressed its support and endorsement. The engagement also strengthened the relationship between the campaign team and the family, and gave direct interaction with key family members and community dignitaries.

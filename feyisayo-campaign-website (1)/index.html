Vision & mission

Government that works for the people.

To build an empowered, prosperous and united Okitipupa Constituency II where every community has a strong voice, every citizen has access to meaningful opportunities, and development is driven by effective representation, accountability and inclusive leadership.

Our vision

A constituency where young people succeed, women are empowered, local businesses thrive and every community receives purposeful representation.

Our mission

To transform political presence into measurable impact through legislation, advocacy, strategic engagement and direct community initiatives.

The central principle

Representation must produce results.

The 2027 election is a turning point. We are committed to a new standard built on accessibility, accountability, development, empowerment and service to the people.

    Who is Dr. Emmanuel Feyisayo?

    He is the African Democratic Congress (ADC) candidate for the House of Assembly seat for Okitipupa Constituency II in 2027.

    What is “The Almighty Shift”?

    It is the campaign’s call for a turning point: representation measured by results, not presence alone.

    How can I reach the campaign?

    Message on WhatsApp or call 0904 267 5152, or follow the campaign on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X.

    Which communities does the campaign cover?

    All seven communities of Okitipupa Constituency II, with every ward and demographic given a voice in development.

How It Works

From application to first dividend.

01

Apply for membership

Submit your particulars and agree to the cooperative's founding terms — pooled savings, mutual growth, and one-member-one-vote governance.

02

Fund your holdings

Deposit into the shared pool at your own pace. Your balance is tracked line-by-line on the co-op's open ledger.

03

Vote, borrow, earn

Cast ballots on cooperative decisions, draw fair-rate loans against your equity, and collect compounding quarterly dividends.
